# Entrada administrativa

Scope: `/admin` before authorization; mode Operate. Audience: league directors signing into the editorial workspace. Existing Supabase Auth and database authorization remain functional.

User-pinned composition: two personalized devices on the left, compact login on the right. Black and white. Flipper Zero and HackRF One carry LASETE's emblem in custom illustrative product art. No social sign-in, signup, app-store promotion or footer directory. The memorable detail is the league logo physically integrated into the hardware.

Desktop shows the full hardware composition and graphite login frame. Mobile stacks the devices above the form; short screens omit the illustration. At standard tested sizes the entire entrance fits without scroll. At extreme height constraints or zoom, preserve reachability instead of clipping controls.

Verified: build, authentication states, desktop/mobile visual review and viewport checks at 1440×900, 1366×768, 1280×720, 1024×768, 768×1024, 390×844, 375×667 and 320×568. No unresolved design decisions.
